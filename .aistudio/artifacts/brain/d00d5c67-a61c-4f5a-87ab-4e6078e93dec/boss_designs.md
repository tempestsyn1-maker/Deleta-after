# Guardian Character Design Specifications

## Floor 6: Elyndor, Keeper of the Last Hymn
- **Aesthetic**: Solemn, ancient, melancholic.
- **Lore**: A former priest who swore an oath to guard the last sacred hymns when the sanctuary fell silent. Their spirit is bound to the hall, their body a ghostly humanoid form clad in frayed purple vestments.
- **Visuals**: Tall, ethereal, translucent purple robes, floating sacred hymn book, faint glowing halo.
- **Combat Mechanics**: Concentric sacred waves, psalm seals, spiritual chorus.

## Floor 7: Zadkiel, Keeper of the Ashen Reliquary
- **Aesthetic**: Ancient, armored, reverent.
- **Lore**: The warden who sacrificed their life to defend the vault of lost names. Clad in tarnished silver and gold plate, their spirit animates the armor.
- **Visuals**: Imposing armored figure, heavy silver and gold armor, glowing relic shield, ancient sword.
- **Combat Mechanics**: Ashen bastion shielding, heavy slams, relic blade barrage.

## Floor 8: Sariel, Warden of the Veiled Scripture
- **Aesthetic**: Mysterious, celestial, distorted.
- **Lore**: A librarian who sought forbidden truth, now bound to the chapel, guarding scriptures corrupted by void energy.
- **Visuals**: Shrouded in celestial but void-tinged robes, hovering, holding floating pages of distorted scripture.
- **Combat Mechanics**: Scripture seals, veiled teleportation, volatile orb volleys.

## Floor 9: Seraphel, Sentinel of the Final Seal
- **Aesthetic**: Radiant, imposing, divine.
- **Lore**: The last line of defense before the gate, imbued with too much celestial light, now barely maintaining their sanity.
- **Visuals**: Blindingly radiant, four wings, celestial spear, glowing golden eyes.
- **Combat Mechanics**: Final seal judgement, celestial thrusts, starlight supernovas.

## Floor 10: Oriphiel, The Last Warden
- **Aesthetic**: Solemn, grim, monumental.
- **Lore**: The final guardian, aware that their defeat seals the sanctuary's fate. They fight not with malice, but with a tragic sense of duty.
- **Visuals**: Towering humanoid form, masterwork armor, massive greatblade, blue warding aura.
- **Combat Mechanics**: Warden's Aegis Bulwark, sanctuary gate collapse (arena-wide slam).
